About The Merchants

A Rapidly Growing
Salt Supplier from Lahore

We are The Merchants, a Lahore-based export company specializing in premium Himalayan Pink Salt products. Led by Rana Tlal (Manager Sales), we serve international buyers across Europe, North America, Asia, and the Middle East.

Our products are sourced directly from the world-famous Khewra Salt Mines — one of the largest and oldest salt mines in the world — ensuring unmatched purity, mineral richness, and authentic Himalayan quality.

We pride ourselves on competitive pricing, flexible packaging, reliable logistics, and a commitment to building long-term trade relationships with every client worldwide.

Shipping Terms

Export & Shipping
Terms Available

We offer flexible shipping and trade terms to suit buyers globally. Please confirm your preferred term, port of delivery, and required quantity when requesting a quote.

01
EXW
Ex-Works

Goods are made available at our facility in Lahore. The buyer is responsible for all transportation, insurance, and customs from our door to their destination. Ideal for buyers with established freight networks.

02
FOB
FOB Karachi

We deliver goods loaded aboard the vessel at Karachi Port. All export customs, inland transport to port, and loading are our responsibility. This is our most popular term for international buyers.

03
C&F
Cost & Freight

We cover cost of goods plus freight charges to the destination port. The buyer handles insurance and import duties. We quote C&F to your nearest port for complete clarity on total landed costs.
